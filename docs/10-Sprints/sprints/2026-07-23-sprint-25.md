## Objective

Create deterministic **outcome feedback** after governed execution — without AI, automation, or a new outcome store.

After Sprint 24, `ExecuteIntentRequest` runs mapped commands through `CommandPipeline`. Sprint 25 derives whether those executions completed, failed, or were cancelled by projecting existing audit events.

---

## Architecture Decision

**Option A: Audit-derived outcome projection** (same pattern as Sprint 22 lifecycle)

```
Audit Trail (ExecuteIntentRequest success/failure)
    ↓
classify_execution_outcome_event / outcome_from_audit_event (pure)
    ↓
ExecutionOutcomeService::list_recent
    ↓
GetExecutionOutcomes (governed read)
```

No outcome database, repository, reinforcement learning, or self-improvement.

---

## Persistence Decision

**None.** Outcomes are derived on demand from existing `ExecuteIntentRequest` audit metadata. Durable history remains the audit trail.

Failure audits merge sanitized `error_code` with optional command `audit_failure_metadata` (execution_request flags) — no parallel event system.

---

## Governance Decision

`GetExecutionOutcomes` is **Governed** with **`audit.read`** and **`PermissionSubject::System`**, matching lifecycle/observation reads.

---

## Audit Decision

Success metadata includes:

```json
{
  "execution_request": true,
  "execution_request_id": "execution:…",
  "suggestion_id": "…",
  "intent_id": "…",
  "execution_status": "executed"
}
```

Failure metadata includes `error_code` plus execution_request markers when provided by `ExecuteIntentRequest::audit_failure_metadata`.

---

## IPC Decision

**Deferred.** No UI consumer. `CommandHandler::get_execution_outcomes` is available for future additive IPC.
