## Outcome

A Workspace Decision Queue aggregates pending approvals, intent proposals, blocked actions, and planning continuations into one ordered inbox. Sources remain authoritative; overlay lifecycle is presentation-only.

See [Governed Decision Queue](../../05-AI/GOVERNED-DECISION-QUEUE.md).

2. Trigger proposals appear once  
3. Blocked actions appear  
4. Planning continuations appear

6. Queue cannot approve permissions  
7. Removing a source removes the item  
8. Accept delegates to source subsystem  
9. Intelligence and Work tab share the same queue
