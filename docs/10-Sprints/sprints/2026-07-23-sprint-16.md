## Objective

Expose a derived, governance-aware answer to: **"What can this actor do right now?"**

Without creating a second permission system.

---

## Architecture Decision

**Option A — Derived capability evaluation:**

```
ActorContext
  ↓
Nominal CapabilitySet (from actor type)
  ↓
Policy + PermissionGate evaluation
  ↓
ActionIntentRegistry filtering
  ↓
CapabilityDiscovery
```

No permission database. No stored profiles. Discovery exposes existing authority; it does not grant authority.

---

## Persistence Decision

**No persistence.**

`CapabilityDiscovery` is derived on demand from actor context, nominal capabilities, policy, and the intent registry.

---

## Governance Decision

**`GovernanceClass::Governed`** for all actors.

Capability discovery reveals authorization metadata (sensitive). Required capability: `settings.read`. Non-human actors remain governed via `read_is_governed`; local human reads are also governed because authorization surface is sensitive.

---

## Audit Decision

**No dedicated audit event** for capability discovery reads beyond standard governed-read pipeline behavior when policy requires it.

---

## IPC Decision

**Added `get_actor_capabilities`** — additive read endpoint returning `IpcResponse<CapabilityDiscovery>`. Does not expose policy internals or permission storage details.

---
