## Outcome

A Continuity-aware Attention Engine projects scored, explainable Attention Items from Decision Queue, Continuity, Activity Graph, and WorkflowContext. Intelligence Recommendations and Assistant consume the same model. No authority or execution paths added.

See [Workspace Attention Engine](../../05-AI/WORKSPACE-ATTENTION-ENGINE.md).

## CASE coverage

1. Attention derives only from existing systems  
2. Attention never becomes a second source of truth  
3. Scoring is deterministic  
4. Every item has an explanation  
5. Resolved sources remove attention naturally  
6. Assistant and Intelligence share Attention  
7. Attention survives restart via source regeneration  
8. Attention cannot execute  
9. Attention cannot bypass Permission Gateway  
10. Existing governance surfaces remain green
