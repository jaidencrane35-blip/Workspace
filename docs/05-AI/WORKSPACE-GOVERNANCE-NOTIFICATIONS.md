# Workspace Governance Notification Contract

Sprint 160 — informational governance notifications (architecture only).

**Never execute work. Never grant authority.**

## GovernanceNotificationContract

Kinds: review assigned/reminder, decision recorded, conflict detected, obligation outstanding,
compliance failed, ready for publication, expiry warning.

Includes recipients, trigger events, delivery status, acknowledgement, reminder scheduling, expiry.
