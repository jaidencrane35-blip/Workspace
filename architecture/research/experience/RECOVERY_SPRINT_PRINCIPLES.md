## Why premium products feel like products (not admin)

| Principle | Source pattern | Workspace translation |
|---|---|---|
| One living place | Arc spaces, Spotify home, Discord guild | Atmosphere always present; modes overlay, they do not replace the place |
| Chrome floats above content | Apple Liquid Glass / dock | Glass dock + glass sheets; canvas is solid spatial content |
| Density without reading | Concept boards, Linear | Icons, tiles, relative time, handoff snippet — kill paragraphs |
| Varied visual weight | Concept masonry | Hero / orbit / compact tiles — never identical CRUD rows |
| Interaction density | Linear microstates | Hover lift, focus glow, stage enter — not decorative confetti |
| Intention first | Product Proof + EXP-001 | Handoff leads; window/process metadata behind Inspect |
| Honest emptiness | Notion + Roadmap | Ghost structure only; no fake activity / AI / ambient |

## Explicit refusals (concept board temptations)

AI chat rails, CPU gauges, audio mixers, live desktop thumbnails, automation
toggles, fabricated activity — EXP-C-N01–N07. Not Product Proof.
